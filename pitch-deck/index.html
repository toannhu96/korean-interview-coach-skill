The problem

Good candidates lose interviews even when they have the right experience.

Vietnamese and foreign candidates often prepare vocabulary, but Korean-company interviews also judge reliability, teamwork, ownership, preparation, and trust.

01

Experience does not become evidence.

Strong work stories sound vague, generic, or unsupported.

02

Fluency is not the only signal.

Grammar tools improve sentences, not interview trust.

03

Company research stays unused.

Facts do not turn into role-specific answer strategy.

Core insight

The gap is not English fluency. It is interview trust.

Most prep tools help candidates sound more fluent. Korean Interview Coach helps them sound more trustworthy by converting real work experience into clear, role-specific English answers.
