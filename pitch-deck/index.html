The problem

Good candidates fail for invisible reasons.

Vietnamese and foreign candidates often prepare vocabulary, but Korean-company interviews judge trust, evidence, and fit.

01

Clear enough is not interview-ready.

Long answers hide signal and make English harder to follow.

02

Ownership is miscalibrated.

Answers sound too passive or too arrogant.

03

Company prep stays generic.

Facts do not become role-specific interview strategy.

Core insight

The gap is not English fluency. It is interview trust.

Korean employers often look for reliability, preparation, teamwork, respectful ownership, and clear communication. Foreign candidates need coaching that turns their real experience into those signals.
