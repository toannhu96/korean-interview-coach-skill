# Question Bank

Ask one question at a time. Choose questions based on the candidate profile, target company, and known weakness.

## Warmup

1. Tell me about yourself and why you are interested in this role.
2. Why do you want to work for a Korean company?
3. What kind of team environment helps you do your best work?
4. What should we know about you beyond your resume?

## Korean-Market Motivation

1. Why are you interested in our company specifically?
2. What do you understand about working with Korean teams?
3. How would you adapt to a different work culture?
4. What concerns do you think a Korean company might have about hiring a foreign candidate?
5. How do you build trust with colleagues from another culture?

## Behavioral

1. Tell me about a time you had to work with a difficult teammate.
2. Tell me about a time you made a mistake at work.
3. Describe a time when you received critical feedback.
4. Tell me about a time you had to meet a tight deadline.
5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or senior colleague.
6. Describe a project where you had to coordinate with multiple stakeholders.
7. Tell me about a time you improved a process.
8. Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly.

## English Communication

1. Explain a complex project you worked on in simple terms.
2. Tell me about your strongest professional achievement in two minutes.
3. Give me a 60-second answer: why should we hire you?
4. Explain a failure without blaming anyone else.
5. Walk me through a decision you made and the tradeoffs you considered.

## Engineering

1. Tell me about a technical problem you solved under pressure.
2. Describe a time you handled a production issue or urgent bug.
3. How do you make technical decisions when requirements are unclear?
4. Tell me about a time you improved code quality or system reliability.
5. How do you communicate technical tradeoffs to non-technical teammates?

## Product / Project Management

1. Tell me about a product or project decision you influenced.
2. How do you prioritize when stakeholders disagree?
3. Describe a time you used data to make a decision.
4. Tell me about a launch that did not go as planned.
5. How would you align a Korean HQ team and a local Vietnam team?

## Sales / Business Development

1. Tell me about a time you built trust with a difficult customer.
2. How do you handle rejection from prospects?
3. Describe a time you managed a long sales or partnership process.
4. How would you explain our value to Vietnamese customers?
5. Tell me about a time you had to negotiate while preserving the relationship.

## Operations / Support

1. Tell me about a time you handled an urgent customer or internal issue.
2. Describe a process you improved.
3. How do you stay accurate when work is repetitive or high-volume?
4. Tell me about a time you escalated a problem.
5. How do you balance speed and quality?

## Design / Marketing / Content

1. Tell me about a campaign or design decision you made based on user insight.
2. How do you respond to feedback from senior stakeholders?
3. Describe a time your first idea did not work.
4. How do you adapt content or design for a Korean brand or audience?
5. Tell me about a measurable result from your creative work.

## Pressure Follow-Ups

Use these after an answer, not as first questions:

- What exactly was your personal contribution?
- What would your manager say you could have done better?
- Why did that result matter to the business?
- What tradeoff did you make?
- If you joined our team, how would this experience help you here?
- How did you communicate this to senior stakeholders?
- What did you learn that changed how you work now?
