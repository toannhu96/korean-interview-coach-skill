# Interview Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Use half-points only if needed.

## 1. English Clarity

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Hard to understand; grammar or pronunciation issues block meaning. |
| 2 | Understandable in parts, but many unclear sentences or confusing phrasing. |
| 3 | Mostly clear; some grammar issues, but the message is understandable. |
| 4 | Clear, professional English with minor errors. |
| 5 | Crisp, natural, interview-ready English. |

Coaching target: simple English that sounds confident. Do not push advanced vocabulary if it reduces clarity.

## 2. Answer Structure

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | No clear answer; stream of consciousness. |
| 2 | Some relevant points, but the listener must work to follow. |
| 3 | Basic structure; beginning, middle, and end are visible. |
| 4 | Clear structure with strong transitions and concise flow. |
| 5 | Sharp answer; headline, evidence, impact, and learning are easy to follow. |

Coaching target: use a simple structure: direct answer -> context -> action -> result -> learning/fit.

## 3. Evidence and Specificity

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Generic claims with no example. |
| 2 | Example exists but lacks detail, numbers, or concrete action. |
| 3 | Specific example but impact or personal role is incomplete. |
| 4 | Clear action and result, with numbers or strong proof. |
| 5 | Strong evidence, candidate ownership, tradeoff, result, and learning. |

Coaching target: replace vague effort with concrete proof.

## 4. Role Relevance

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Does not answer the question or fit the target role. |
| 2 | Loosely related but misses the main signal. |
| 3 | Relevant but not tailored to the company or role. |
| 4 | Strong fit to role expectations. |
| 5 | Directly maps the story to what the target Korean company likely needs. |

Coaching target: every answer should make the interviewer think, "This person can do this job here."

## 5. Korean Professional Fit

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Sounds careless, arrogant, disrespectful, or unaware of team context. |
| 2 | Shows effort but misses hierarchy, coordination, or reliability signals. |
| 3 | Professional enough, but could show stronger teamwork and responsibility. |
| 4 | Respectful, team-aware, and reliable. |
| 5 | Excellent balance of ownership, humility, preparation, and team fit. |

Coaching target: communicate accountability and respect without becoming passive.

## 6. Confidence and Humility Balance

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Either too passive or too arrogant. |
| 2 | Unclear ownership; hides impact or overclaims. |
| 3 | Balanced in parts, but inconsistent. |
| 4 | Confident about contribution while acknowledging team and learning. |
| 5 | Strong, mature, trustworthy self-presentation. |

Coaching target: "I owned X, coordinated with Y, and learned Z."

## Overall Read

- **Strong Hire**: Mostly 4-5, no major trust or clarity gaps.
- **Hire**: Mostly 3-4, coachable gaps.
- **Mixed**: Uneven; one or two serious gaps affect confidence.
- **No Hire**: Low clarity, weak evidence, poor fit, or trust concerns.
