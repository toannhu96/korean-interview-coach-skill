# Answer Patterns

Use these patterns to help candidates produce clear English answers calibrated for Korean-company interviews.

## Default Structure

Use this for most behavioral answers:

```text
Direct answer: [one-sentence answer to the question]
Context: [brief situation and why it mattered]
Action: [what I personally did]
Result: [measurable or observable outcome]
Learning / Fit: [what I learned and why it matters for this role]
```

## Korean-Market Strong Answer Formula

```text
I understood the team/company goal.
I aligned with the right people.
I took clear ownership of my part.
I delivered or learned something measurable.
I can apply that behavior in this Korean-company role.
```

## Rewriting Rules

When improving an answer:

1. Keep the candidate's real experience. Do not invent facts.
2. Simplify English before making it impressive.
3. Add ownership if the answer overuses "we."
4. Add teamwork if the answer overuses "I."
5. Add numbers only when the candidate provided them or they are clearly marked as placeholders.
6. Remove unnecessary apology, filler, or long background.
7. End with role fit, not a generic life lesson.

## Weak Answer Patterns and Fixes

### Too vague

Weak:

```text
I worked hard and helped my team finish the project successfully.
```

Fix:

```text
My role was to coordinate the testing process and remove blockers for the team. We had only two weeks before launch, so I created a daily issue tracker, followed up with each owner, and escalated two risks early. As a result, we launched on time with no critical bugs reported in the first week.
```

### Too modest

Weak:

```text
My team did the project and I supported them.
```

Fix:

```text
The project was a team effort, and my specific role was to own the customer data analysis. I found that most complaints came from one onboarding step, shared the insight with the team, and proposed a simpler flow. That helped us reduce repeated support questions.
```

### Too arrogant

Weak:

```text
I was the only person who could solve it, so I fixed everything myself.
```

Fix:

```text
I took ownership of the issue because I had the most context, but I kept the team aligned. I diagnosed the root cause, asked one engineer to verify the fix, and updated my manager before release. That helped us solve the problem quickly without creating extra risk.
```

### Too long

Weak symptom: candidate explains company history, team structure, and background before answering.

Fix:

```text
The short answer is yes, I have handled this before. In my last role, the main challenge was [X]. My responsibility was [Y]. I solved it by [Z], and the result was [impact].
```

## Feedback Language in Vietnamese

Use concise Vietnamese coaching:

- "Ý chính đúng, nhưng câu trả lời chưa đủ bằng chứng."
- "Bạn đang dùng 'we' quá nhiều, nên interviewer chưa thấy phần ownership của bạn."
- "Câu này nên ngắn hơn. Công ty Hàn thường đánh giá cao sự rõ ràng và đáng tin."
- "Bạn cần thể hiện khiêm tốn, nhưng không được giấu impact."
- "Đây là style difference, không phải lỗi cá nhân. Mình sẽ chỉnh để hợp context công ty Hàn hơn."

## Improved Answer Output Rules

Provide two English versions:

1. **Corrected version**: Cleaner English, same content.
2. **Korean-market stronger version**: Adds structure, responsibility, teamwork, and role fit.

If facts are missing, use bracketed placeholders:

```text
[add metric if available]
[name the stakeholder]
[specific result]
```

Do not invent employer names, metrics, Korean phrases, or interviewer expectations.
