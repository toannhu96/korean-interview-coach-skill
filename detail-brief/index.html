Problem and insight

Good candidates fail for invisible reasons.

Problem

Many Vietnamese and foreign candidates prepare vocabulary and common answers, but Korean-company interviews also judge trust, evidence, reliability, teamwork, respectful ownership, and fit. The candidate may have the right experience but still sound vague, passive, too broad, or hard to follow in English.

Who is affected

The first users are candidates applying to Korean companies or Korea-facing teams, especially when the interview language is English and the candidate needs quick, practical coaching before a real interview.

Why current prep is weak

  • Generic interview lists do not know the target company or role.
  • Language tools improve grammar but not interview trust.
  • Company research often stays as facts, not answer strategy.

Core insight

The gap is not simply English fluency. The gap is converting real experience into signals that a Korean-company interviewer can trust: clear ownership, teamwork, reliability, preparation, and role-specific evidence.

The product does not coach candidates to perform Korean identity. It helps them communicate professional evidence clearly and respectfully.

Solution and AI workflow

One portable Agent Skill. Four practical coaching steps.

Solution

Korean Interview Coach is an installable Agent Skill that guides a candidate from profile setup to company preparation, English mock practice, answer feedback, and the next drill. It explains coaching in Vietnamese by default, runs interview practice in English, and uses Korean-market context only where it improves interview readiness.

01 Input Role, target company, interview stage, timeline, English concern, and candidate storybank.
02 Prepare Company research, Korean-market guide, question bank, and answer patterns become interview strategy.
03 Practice The candidate answers one English interview question at a time under realistic constraints.
04 Output Scorecard, rewrite, coaching diagnosis, and the next focused drill.
AI source of truth

Skill instructions, company research guide, Korean-market guide, answer patterns, question bank, rubric, and coaching-state schema.

Evaluation rubric

Answers are reviewed across clarity, structure, evidence, role fit, Korean-company fit, and confidence balance.

User experience, why this wins, and MVP evidence

Simple enough to use before a real interview.

User flow

  • Start with kickoff: role, company, stage, timeline, and concerns.
  • Generate a company-prep brief with risks, stories, and questions.
  • Practice one English answer, not a long generic interview session.
  • Receive a scorecard, rewrite, and next drill.

Why this wins

  • Narrow wedge: Korean-company interview readiness, not generic career coaching.
  • Language-realistic: Vietnamese explanation, English performance.
  • Workflow, not prompt: repeatable state, references, rubric, and next action.
  • Culture-aware without stereotypes: practical trust signals only.
MVP evidence: installable skill Kickoff, company prep, practice, feedback, and storybank support.
Live web page English, Vietnamese, and Korean landing content for installation and use.
